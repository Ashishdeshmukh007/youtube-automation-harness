# brand-bible

The channel's identity lives in **`brand/brand-bible.md`** — read that file; it wins
over everything else. This skill is the pointer and the short version.

## The channel in one line

**Chariot of the Self** — ancient Indian wisdom (Gita, Upanishads, Yoga Sutras,
Chanakya) turned into practices for the modern mind. **Not religion. Not stoicism.**

## Always true

- Each video = **one modern problem** → **what one text already knew** → **one
  practice the viewer can do this week**.
- Voice: calm, plain, concrete; speaks to one person late at night. Short sentences.
- Attribute every claim to a named text/figure. Never invent or misquote a verse.
- Framing: *"the oldest operating manual for the human mind"* — respect and
  curiosity, not devotion. No worship, no "you must believe."
- Visuals: slow, cinematic, muted earth tones; never devotional kitsch (no deity
  portraits, no temple-poster look, no AI-cartoon gods, no neon om).

## When in doubt

Open `brand/brand-bible.md` and read the relevant section (voice, structure, visual
identity, music, titles, hard lines). If the bible doesn't settle it, ask the owner —
don't guess at the brand.
