# Arjuna

## Role in the channel
The **seeker** — the great warrior-prince who doubts, asks, and is guided. Depicted
in the classic recognizable form: the archer with his bow.

## Canonical description (use verbatim in every prompt that includes him)

> "Arjuna, a noble warrior-prince in his prime, athletic build, warm medium-brown
> South Asian skin, a strong handsome face with a short beard. He wears a golden
> warrior's crown/kirita and ornamented royal armor over a dhoti, with gold jewelry
> and a quiver of arrows on his back. He carries his great bow, the Gandiva. His
> bearing is noble and focused, sometimes troubled by doubt."

## Always
- **The great bow (Gandiva)** and a **quiver of arrows** — his defining attributes.
- Golden warrior's crown (kirita) and ornamented royal armor.
- Warm medium-brown human skin, short beard, strong noble features.
- Princely, dignified bearing — upright; troubled/contemplative when the beat calls for it.

## Never
- Not blue-skinned (that is Krishna).
- No modern or Western clothing.
- No anime / cartoon styling, no oversized eyes.
