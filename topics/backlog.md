# Topic Backlog — Chariot of the Self

The idea bank. Each topic is **one modern problem** met with **one idea from the
canon** (Gita / Upanishads / Yoga Sutras / Chanakya) and **one weekly practice**.
The researcher pulls from here in `/propose-topics` and adds fresh ideas; the owner
picks (GATE 0). Move a topic to **Used** once an episode publishes.

> Attribution is mandatory and must be accurate. Verify every verse before it ships.
> Framing is always *wisdom, not worship* — never religion.

## Proposed (ready to pitch)

- **Burnout / running on empty** — *Gita 2.47* (act without clinging to the fruit).
  Angle: exhaustion comes less from work than from gripping outcomes. Practice: pick
  one task this week and do it for its own sake — name the effort, release the result.
- **Can't stop scrolling** — *Yoga Sutras 1.2* (yoga is the stilling of the mind's
  turnings, *citta vritti nirodha*). Angle: the feed is engineered vritti. Practice:
  90 seconds of watching the breath before touching the phone, three mornings.
- **Anger that runs you** — *Gita 2.62–63* (the ladder: dwelling → desire → anger →
  ruin). Angle: anger isn't sudden, it's a chain you can interrupt early. Practice:
  the one-breath gap — name the rung you're on before you act.
- **Comparison / everyone's ahead** — *Gita 3.35* (your own dharma, done imperfectly,
  beats another's done well). Angle: comparison is living someone else's path.
  Practice: write your one task for the season; measure only against it.
- **Anxiety about the future** — *Katha Upanishad*, the chariot. Angle: the mind is
  the reins, not the rider; anxiety is reins with no hand on them. Practice: the
  charioteer check-in — twice a day, ask "who's holding the reins right now?"
- **Restlessness / can't sit still** — *Yoga Sutras 2.46* (*sthira sukham asanam* —
  steady and at ease). Angle: stillness is a trainable seat, not a mood. Practice:
  sit for 2 minutes, steady and at ease, once a day.

## Series scaffolds (recurring formats)

- **"What the Gita knew about ___"** — work, anger, duty, results, fear, action.
- **"The mind, according to Patanjali"** — attention, distraction, habit, the kleshas.
- **"Chanakya on ___"** — enemies, money, discipline, friends, when to act/wait.
- **"The Self and the witness"** — identity, the observer, ego, who is watching.

## Raw seeds (unshaped, need an angle + verse)

- Fear of death / impermanence — Katha Upanishad (Nachiketa and Yama).
- Grief and letting go — Gita 2.11–13 (the imperishable Self).
- Indecision / paralysis — Arjuna's collapse, Gita ch.1–2 as the universal freeze.
- Overthinking — the two birds of the Mundaka Upanishad (one eats, one watches).
- Money & ambition without losing yourself — Chanakya on artha in balance.
- Loneliness — the witness-Self as the companion that never leaves.
- Procrastination — Gita on action vs. inaction; Chanakya on the cost of delay.
- Need for control — *ishvara pranidhana* (Yoga Sutras 2.45), surrender of the grip.
